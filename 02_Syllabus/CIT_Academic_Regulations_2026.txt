COIMBATORE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (CIT), COIMBATORE
ACADEMIC REGULATIONS, ATTENDANCE & EXAMINATION RULES 2026
===================================================================

1. CHOICE BASED CREDIT SYSTEM (CBCS)
CIT Coimbatore follows the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) for all undergraduate (B.E. / B.Tech) and postgraduate (M.E. / M.Tech / MCA) degree programs.
- Minimum credit requirements for B.E. / B.Tech (4 Years): 160 to 165 Credits.
- Lateral Entry B.E. / B.Tech (3 Years): 120 to 125 Credits.
- M.E. / M.Tech (2 Years): 70 to 75 Credits.
- MCA (2 Years): 80 to 85 Credits.

2. MANDATORY ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum Attendance: Every student must secure a minimum of 75% attendance in aggregate of all courses in a semester to be eligible to appear for the End Semester Theory and Practical Examinations.
- Condonation of Attendance (65% to 74%):
  Condonation of shortage of attendance between 65% and 74% may be granted by the Principal on genuine medical grounds (supported by medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner) or participation in official sports/NSS/NCC events. Condonation fee as prescribed by the institution must be paid.
- Below 65% Attendance:
  Students securing less than 65% attendance are NOT eligible to appear for semester examinations under any circumstances. They are detained and must re-register and repeat the semester in the subsequent academic year.

3. CONTINUOUS ASSESSMENT & EVALUATION SYSTEM
Assessment Weightage:
- Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT / Internal Evaluation): 40% Weightage.
- End Semester Theory & Practical Examination: 60% Weightage.

Internal Assessment Components (40 Marks):
- Continuous Assessment Test 1 (CAT 1): 15 Marks.
- Continuous Assessment Test 2 (CAT 2): 15 Marks.
- Assignments / Quizzes / Seminar / Mini Project: 10 Marks.

4. GRADING SYSTEM & CGPA CALCULATION
Letter Grades and Grade Points:
- O (Outstanding): Grade Point 10 (90 - 100 Marks)
- A+ (Excellent): Grade Point 9 (80 - 89 Marks)
- A (Very Good): Grade Point 8 (70 - 79 Marks)
- B+ (Good): Grade Point 7 (60 - 69 Marks)
- B (Average): Grade Point 6 (50 - 59 Marks)
- C (Satisfactory): Grade Point 5 (45 - 49 Marks)
- RA (Re-appear / Fail): Grade Point 0 (< 45 Marks)
- W (Withdrawn): 0

Formula:
- SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) = SUM(Credit * Grade Point) / SUM(Credits in Semester)
- CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) = SUM(Credit * Grade Point) / SUM(Total Credits Earned)

5. REVALUATION & ARREAR EXAMINATIONS
- Arrear Examinations: Conducted during regular semester exam periods (Odd semester arrears in Nov/Dec, Even semester arrears in April/May).
- Supplementary Examinations: Conducted for final year students after 8th semester to enable early completion of degree.
- Revaluation Procedure: Students can apply for photocopy of answer scripts within 7 days of results declaration. Revaluation applications must be submitted with prescribed fee per paper to the Office of Controller of Examinations (COE).
