1. B.E. COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING (CSE) & B.E. CSE (AIML)
Semester I:
- Professional English & Communication
- Matrices and Calculus
- Engineering Physics
- Engineering Chemistry
- Python Programming & Problem Solving
- Python Programming Laboratory
- Physics and Chemistry Laboratory

Semester II:
- Technical English
- Differential Equations & Complex Variables
- Basic Electrical & Electronics Engineering
- Programming in C & Data Structures
- Engineering Graphics
- Data Structures Laboratory

Semester III:
- Discrete Mathematics & Graph Theory
- Object Oriented Programming in C++/Java
- Digital Logic Design & Computer Architecture
- Database Management Systems (DBMS)
- Software Engineering & Agile Principles
- DBMS & OOP Laboratory

Semester IV:
- Probability, Statistics & Random Processes
- Design and Analysis of Algorithms
- Operating Systems & Linux Architecture
- Theory of Computation & Automata
- Computer Networks & Protocols
- Operating Systems & Networks Lab

Semester V (3rd Year CSE & AIML):
- Artificial Intelligence Principles & Search Strategies
- Machine Learning Algorithms & Supervised/Unsupervised Learning
- Web Technologies & Full Stack Frameworks
- Computer Networks & Distributed Systems
- Professional Elective I (Deep Learning / Cloud Computing / Cybersecurity)
- AI & Machine Learning Laboratory
- Web Development Laboratory

Semester VI (3rd Year CSE & AIML):
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) & Text Mining
- Computer Vision & Image Processing
- Big Data Analytics & Distributed Computing (Hadoop/Spark)
- Open Elective I (Management / Humanities)
- Professional Elective II (Reinforcement Learning / MLOps)
- Mini Project & Innovation Lab

Semester VII (4th Year CSE & AIML):
- Deep Learning & Neural Networks
- Information & Cyber Security
- Professional Elective III & IV
- Open Elective II
- Project Work Phase-I

Semester VIII (4th Year CSE & AIML):
- Professional Elective V
- Project Work Phase-II & Internship Demonstration

2. B.E. ELECTRONICS AND COMMUNICATION ENGINEERING (ECE)
- Key Subjects: Electronic Devices & Circuits, Signals and Systems, Analog & Digital Communication, Microprocessors & Microcontrollers (8051/ARM), VLSI Design, Embedded Systems, Antennas and Wave Propagation, Wireless Communication, RF Systems, Digital Signal Processing (DSP).

3. B.E. ELECTRICAL AND ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING (EEE)
- Key Subjects: Electric Circuit Analysis, Electromagnetic Fields, Electrical Machines I & II, Power Systems Analysis, Power Electronics, Control Systems, Microgrid Technology, High Voltage Engineering, Renewable Energy Systems.

4. B.E. MECHANICAL ENGINEERING
- Key Subjects: Engineering Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Manufacturing Technology, Kinematics & Dynamics of Machinery, Heat and Mass Transfer, Design of Machine Elements, Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulic Machinery, CAD/CAM/CIM, Mechatronics & Automation.

5. B.E. CIVIL ENGINEERING
- Key Subjects: Engineering Geology, Mechanics of Solids, Surveying, Soil Mechanics & Foundation Engineering, Structural Analysis, Concrete Technology, Transportation Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Reinforced Concrete Structures.
