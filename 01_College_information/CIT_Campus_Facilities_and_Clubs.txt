===================================================================
COIMBATORE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (CIT), COIMBATORE
CAMPUS FACILITIES, HOSTELS, CLUBS AND STUDENT LIFE
===================================================================

1. CENTRAL LIBRARY
- The CIT Central Library is a 3-story modern facility with a vast collection of over 100,000 volumes of books, national/international journals, IEEE Xplore digital library access, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, and e-books.
- Digital Library wing equipped with high-speed internet PCs for online research, NPTEL course viewing, and e-journal reading.
- Open Hours: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on working days.

2. HOSTEL FACILITIES
- Men's Hostels: Multiple hostel blocks (Block A, B, C, D) inside campus accommodating over 1,500 male students. Rooms available on single, double, and triple sharing basis.
- Women's Hostels: Secure campus residential facility for over 1,000 female students with 24/7 security, CCTV surveillance, resident wardens, and medical attendance.
- Facilities in Hostels: Wi-Fi connectivity, RO drinking water, study halls, recreation rooms with TV, gymnasium, laundry facilities, and indoor sports facilities.
- Mess: Hygienic vegetarian and non-vegetarian dining halls serving nutritious South Indian and North Indian food.

3. SPORTS & ATHLETICS
- Facilities include standard 400-meter athletics track, Football field, Cricket ground, Basketball courts with floodlights, Volleyball courts, Badminton courts, and Table Tennis rooms.
- Annual Inter-Collegiate Sports Meets, Intramural Tournaments, and Coaching programs by qualified Physical Directors.

4. HEALTH CARE & MEDICAL CENTER
- Campus Health Center with full-time medical officer and nursing staff for regular checkups, first aid, and emergency medical assistance.
- 24/7 Ambulance facility available on campus for immediate transportation to nearby multi-specialty hospitals in Coimbatore.

5. CLUBS, SOCIETIES & EXTRA-CURRICULAR ACTIVITIES
- Rotaract Club of CIT: Community service, blood donation camps, social awareness campaigns.
- NSS (National Service Scheme) & NCC (National Cadet Corps): Army wing & Air wing units developing discipline, patriotism, and social service.
- IEEE Student Branch: Technical workshops, hackathons, coding contests, paper presentations.
- Fine Arts Club & Drama Club: Cultural performances, music, dance, theater, and arts competitions.
- Literary and Debating Society (LDS): English & Tamil debating, quiz competitions, creative writing.
- CIT Open House & Cyber Fest: Annual national technical fests showcasing student projects, robotics, and innovative prototypes.
- YUGAM / CIT Cultural Fest: Annual inter-collegiate cultural fest celebrating music, dance, art, and youth culture.
