COIMBATORE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (CIT) - COLLEGE OVERVIEW & GOVERNANCE
===================================================================

Institution Name: Coimbatore Institute of Technology (CIT)
Location: Civil Aerodrome Post, Avinashi Road, Coimbatore - 641014, Tamil Nadu, India.
Founding Year: Established in 1956 by V. Rangaswamy Naidu Educational Trust (VRET).
Affiliation & Status: Government-Aided Autonomous Institution affiliated to Anna University, Chennai.
Accreditation & Approvals: Approved by AICTE, Accredited by NAAC with 'A' Grade, NBA Accredited engineering programs.
Motto: "Progress Through Technology"

Vision:
To impart high quality technical education and research to students, nurturing ethical and innovative leaders who contribute to global industrial and societal development.

Mission:
1. Provide state-of-the-art academic infrastructure and quality learning environment.
2. Foster research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and industry-institute collaboration.
3. Cultivate ethical values, leadership qualities, and social responsibility among engineering students.

Key Highlights of CIT Coimbatore:
- Ranking & Legacy: Ranked among top engineering colleges in Tamil Nadu and nationally in NIRF rankings.
- Campus Size: Sprawling 25+ acre lush green campus in Peelamedu, Coimbatore.
- Industry Partnerships: MoUs with leading multinational companies including Bosch, TCS, L&T, IBM, Qualcomm, and Texas Instruments.
- Autonomous Status: Autonomous since 1987, allowing CIT to design modern, industry-relevant curriculum aligned with global engineering standards.
