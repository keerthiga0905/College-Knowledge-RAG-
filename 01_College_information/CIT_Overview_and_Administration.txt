1. ABOUT CIT COIMBATORE
Coimbatore Institute of Technology (CIT), located in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, is one of the premier government-aided engineering institutions in South India. Founded in 1956 by Sri. V. Rangaswamy Naidu Educational Trust, CIT has been a pioneer in technical education, research, and industry-institute collaboration for nearly seven decades.
The institution was granted Autonomous Status by Anna University and UGC in 1987, enabling CIT to continuously update its curriculum, introduce modern specializations, and maintain high standards of technical education.

2. VISION AND MISSION OF CIT
Vision:
To impart high quality technical education and research to students, nurturing ethical, innovative, and socially responsible leaders who contribute to global industrial and societal development.

Mission:
- Provide state-of-the-art academic infrastructure and a supportive learning environment.
- Foster research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and industry-institute interaction.
- Cultivate ethical values, leadership qualities, and social responsibility among engineering graduates.
- Continually update curriculum in line with emerging technologies and industry needs.

4. ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY & PROCESS
Undergraduate (B.E. / B.Tech):
- Admissions are conducted through Single Window Counseling by TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions) based on Class 12 Higher Secondary Marks (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics).
- Management Quota admissions follow Tamil Nadu Self Financing Engineering Colleges Consortium merit guidelines.
- Lateral Entry B.E. / B.Tech (Direct 2nd Year admission for Diploma holders and B.Sc graduates) is conducted through TNEA Lateral Entry counseling.

Postgraduate (M.E. / M.Tech / MCA):
- Admissions via TANCET (Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test) / CEETA-PG conducted by Anna University or GATE scores.
- MCA admissions require a valid bachelor's degree with Mathematics at 10+2 or Graduation level and TANCET score.
