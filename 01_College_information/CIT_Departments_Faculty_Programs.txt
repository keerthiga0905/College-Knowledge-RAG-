COIMBATORE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (CIT), COIMBATORE
DEPARTMENTS, FACULTY, AND ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

1. ACADEMIC DEPARTMENTS AT CIT
Coimbatore Institute of Technology houses 12 academic departments offering B.E., B.Tech, M.E., M.Tech, MCA, M.Sc, and Ph.D. degree programs:

1. Department of Civil Engineering (Est. 1956)
   - Programs Offered: B.E. Civil Engineering, M.E. Structural Engineering, M.E. Environmental Engineering & Management, Ph.D.
   - Key Focus: Structural Analysis, Geotechnical Engineering, Environmental Management, Smart City Infrastructure.

2. Department of Mechanical Engineering (Est. 1956)
   - Programs Offered: B.E. Mechanical Engineering, M.E. Heat Power Engineering, M.E. Advanced Manufacturing Technology, Ph.D.
   - Key Focus: Robotics, Thermal Engineering, CAD/CAM/CAE, Additive Manufacturing.

3. Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering - EEE (Est. 1956)
   - Programs Offered: B.E. Electrical and Electronics Engineering, M.E. Power Electronics & Drives, M.E. Embedded and Real Time Systems, Ph.D.
   - Key Focus: Renewable Energy Systems, Smart Grids, Electric Vehicles, Power Electronics.

4. Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering - ECE (Est. 1968)
   - Programs Offered: B.E. Electronics and Communication Engineering, M.E. Communication Engineering, M.E. VLSI Design, Ph.D.
   - Key Focus: VLSI Design, Wireless Communications, IoT, Signal Processing, Microelectronics.

5. Department of Computer Science and Engineering - CSE (Est. 1986)
   - Programs Offered: B.E. Computer Science and Engineering, M.E. Computer Science and Engineering, Ph.D.
   - Key Focus: Cloud Computing, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, Big Data Analytics.

6. Department of Information Technology - IT (Est. 2001)
   - Programs Offered: B.Tech Information Technology, Ph.D.
   - Key Focus: Full Stack Development, Network Security, Web Technologies, Distributed Systems.

7. Department of Chemical Engineering (Est. 1994)
   - Programs Offered: B.Tech Chemical Engineering, M.Tech Chemical Engineering, Ph.D.
   - Key Focus: Process Modeling, Biochemical Engineering, Reaction Engineering, Polymer Technology.

8. Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science - AI & DS / CSE (AIML)
   - Programs Offered: B.Tech Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, B.E. Computer Science & Engineering (AI & ML).
   - Key Focus: Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, Neural Networks, Generative AI.

9. Department of Humanities & Social Sciences
   - Offering Technical English, Communication Skills, Economics, Professional Ethics, and Soft Skills training.

10. Department of Mathematics
    - Advanced Engineering Mathematics, Numerical Methods, Discrete Mathematics, Probability & Statistics.

11. Department of Physics & Department of Chemistry
    - Engineering Physics, Engineering Chemistry, Nanoscience, Materials Characterization Labs.

12. Department of Computer Applications (MCA) & Integrated M.Sc
    - Master of Computer Applications (MCA - 2 Year Program)
    - 5-Year Integrated M.Sc. Data Science, M.Sc. Software Systems, M.Sc. Decision & Computing Sciences.

2. FACULTY & RESEARCH HIGHLIGHTS
- CIT has over 200+ qualified faculty members, over 70% holding Ph.D. degrees from IISc, IITs, NITs, and premier universities.
- Over 500+ research scholars pursuing Ph.D. under Anna University Research Centers at CIT.
- MoUs with Industry Giants: Robert Bosch, Texas Instruments, Qualcomm, IBM, TCS, L&T, Hyundai Motors, National Instruments.
- Specialized Centers of Excellence: Bosch Automotive Electronics Lab, Texas Instruments Innovation Lab, Supercomputing Center.
