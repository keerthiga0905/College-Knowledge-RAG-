COIMBATORE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (CIT), COIMBATORE
FEES STRUCTURE, SCHOLARSHIPS, AND CAREER PLACEMENTS
===================================================================

1. FEE STRUCTURE AT CIT COIMBATORE
Undergraduate B.E. / B.Tech (Per Academic Year):
- Government Aided Streams (Civil, Mech, EEE, ECE):
  - Tuition Fee & Special Fees: Approx ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per year (Government subsidized rate).
  - Development Fee & Exam Fee: Approx ₹8,000 per year.
- Self-Supporting Streams (CSE, IT, Chemical, AI & DS, CSE AIML):
  - Tuition Fee: Approx ₹50,000 to ₹70,000 per year (as per Tamil Nadu Fee Fixation Committee).
  - Special Facilities & Lab Fee: Approx ₹15,000 per year.

Postgraduate M.E. / M.Tech / MCA (Per Academic Year):
- M.E. / M.Tech Aided / Self-Supporting: Approx ₹35,000 to ₹60,000 per year.
- MCA Program: Approx ₹40,000 to ₹55,000 per year.

Hostel & Mess Fees (Per Year):
- Hostel Rent & Maintenance: Approx ₹25,000 per year.
- Mess Deposit & Monthly Charges: Approx ₹35,000 to ₹40,000 per year (Dividing System).

2. SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
- First Generation Graduate Scholarship: Government of Tamil Nadu tuition fee waiver (₹20,000 to ₹25,000 per year) for students who are the first in their family to pursue higher education.
- BC / MBC / DNC Welfare Scholarship: Post-Matric scholarship awarded by Tamil Nadu Backward Classes Welfare Department for eligible students with family income under ₹2.5 Lakhs/year.
- SC / ST State Post-Matric Scholarship: Full tuition fee reimbursement for SC/ST students with annual family income less than ₹2.5 Lakhs.
- AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls: Financial aid of ₹50,000 per year for meritorious girl students admitted to B.E./B.Tech programs.
- AICTE Saksham Scholarship: Financial support for specially-abled students.
- Merit-cum-Means Scholarships: Awarded by CIT Alumni Association (CITAA) and industrial trusts for top-ranking needy students.
